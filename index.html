Amman GTA

The game اللعبة

AmmanGTA is an open-world, adventure-driven game set in the lively streets of Amman, Jordan.

The story follows a character who feels lost and uncertain about their future, dreaming of finding success and their place in the world, only to wake up and realize it was all just a dream. Determined to turn that dream into reality, players explore the city, complete missions, earn money, and discover their own path toward success. Inspired by the everyday life, culture, and atmosphere of Amman, the game combines humor, exploration, and progression into a uniquely Jordanian experience.
